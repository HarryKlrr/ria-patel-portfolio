    How I work

    Design it. Simulate it.
    Then prove it on the bench.

    Every design decision gets checked twice: once in simulation and once in physical testing. Each step below links to where I've done it.

    1. 01

      Design

      Parametric CAD in Fusion 360 and SolidWorks, from variable-thickness sockets to full stent lattices.

      Braided stent model →
    2. 02

      Simulate

      Structural FEA in SimScale, run under the same loads as the real tests so the two can be compared.

      Socket FEA →
    3. 03

      Test

      FDM-printed samples put through tensile, compression and three-point bending on an Instron, to BS ISO standards.

      Materials testing →
    4. 04

      Validate

      Simulation checked against lab data: within 1% in tension and 7.5% in bending for PA12.
